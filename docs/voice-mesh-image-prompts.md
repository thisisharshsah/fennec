# Voice mesh radio: image prompts

Paste the base block at the start of every prompt, then one view line.

## Base block

```
A handheld license-free voice mesh radio, about the size of a chunky smartphone, designed as a premium outdoor gadget. Rounded matte charcoal body with a soft orange rubber bumper around the edges, a short stubby flexible antenna at the top corner, a large textured push-to-talk button on the left side under the thumb, a small round speaker grille on the front with a tiny microphone hole beneath it, and a thin horizontal light bar that shows signal and who is talking. No screen, no text, no logo, no visible seams. A fabric lanyard loop at the bottom and a USB-C port under a rubber flap. Rugged but calm Scandinavian industrial design, premium matte plastics, built for hikers, event crews and rescue teams.
```
