# Room creature: image prompts

Two things to picture: the finished product and the honest first prototype.
Paste the base block at the start of every prompt, then one view line.

## 1. The product

### Base block (creature)

```
A small upright robot creature about 15 cm tall, an original mascot character made as a premium gadget: a big round head on a small round body with stubby feet, two large soft rounded ears that stand up from the head, and a short rounded tail. Matte warm cream body, the face a smooth dark charcoal panel with no eyes or screen: one small round sensor lens where a nose would be and two tiny microphone holes in the ears. Two soft round glowing cheek spots either side of the face. No text, no logo, no visible seams. Calm Scandinavian industrial design, friendly and expressive without a face, premium matte plastics, the posture of a small creature that just heard something: ears up, head tilted.
```

### Base block (stationary cell)

```
A small matte white pebble-shaped sensor about 8 cm across, a smooth dome with a thin soft light ring glowing along its lower seam and a small dark front panel with one round sensor lens and two tiny microphone holes. No text, no logo, no visible seams. Calm Scandinavian industrial design, premium matte plastics.
```

## 2. The prototype

```
A small homemade robot creature about 18 cm tall: a 3D-printed white PLA head and body, two large printed ears each on a blue SG90 micro servo, a small printed tail on another servo, the head on a pan-tilt bracket. A green rectangular STM32 IoT discovery board is mounted inside the open body as its brain with a small servo driver board and jumper wires visible. It sits on a wooden desk beside a laptop showing a terminal window. Honest maker-project photography, natural light, photorealistic, no text overlays.
```

```
First week: the bare green STM32 IoT discovery board on a desk connected by USB to a laptop, a person's hand clapping near it, the laptop terminal showing a line of text. Photorealistic, natural light.
```
