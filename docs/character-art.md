# Creature bot artwork

Fennec appears as a playful desk bot with an ivory ceramic shell, lilac hardware, a glossy navy display and cyan LED expressions. Its antenna fins and cable tail echo the device's expressive joints. Keep its facial features graphical and mechanical.

The app uses the live procedural RealityKit model in large character views and the portrait asset in compact lists. Small portraits keep scrolling light. Mood, cheek colour and joint readings still come from the device. The welcome screen offers a local mood preview.

## Source assets

* `apps/RoomCreature/RoomCreature/Assets.xcassets/CreatureBotPortrait.imageset/creature-bot.png`: transparent full body portrait.
* `apps/RoomCreature/RoomCreature/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/`: square app icon in the platform sizes.
* `apps/RoomCreature/RoomCreature/Views/FennecModelView.swift`: procedural geometry, materials, camera, lighting and joint rig.

The finished image assets are application source resources. Build products and preview captures remain in the existing ignored `build/` and `data/` folders.

## Portrait prompt

Built with the built-in image generation tool, transparent output.

Create one original premium 3D robotic creature desk companion. Give it an oversized rounded squircle ivory ceramic head, recessed glossy midnight navy screen, luminous cyan and periwinkle rounded pill LED eyes with highlights, a small glowing curved smile and lilac cheek pixels. Add two short asymmetric ivory antenna fins with translucent lilac panels, a compact ivory and lilac torso, small rounded robot arms, chunky oval feet, a cyan chest status dot and a short curved cable tail ending in a glowing lilac plug. Use precisely modeled shell rims and hinges, soft satin ceramic, glossy dark glass, clean seams, soft warm studio key light and cool rim light. Show one complete bot near front view on a genuinely transparent square canvas, occupying about 86 percent of the height with clear margins. Keep the design readable at small sizes. No biological eyes, snout, muzzle, fur, paws, clothing, props, words, logos or watermark. Keep the design original.

## Icon prompt

Built with the built-in image generation tool using the portrait as the identity reference, opaque output.

Make a square native app icon portrait of this exact bot, showing its head and a hint of shoulders. Preserve the ivory squircle head, glossy navy display, cyan pill eyes and LED smile, lilac cheek pixels and short ivory and lilac antenna fins. Use a near frontal view with a playful five degree lean. Fit the entire head and fins inside the canvas with eight percent clear margin. Make the face large and readable at small sizes. Preserve the modeled ceramic shell, glass rim, studio illumination and cool rim light. Fill every pixel to the square edges with a very pale lavender studio gradient. No baked rounded mask, border, text, words, logos, props or watermark.
